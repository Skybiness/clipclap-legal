Clipclap is a personal publishing tool operated by Skybiness Clips (TikTok, Instagram and YouTube: @skybiness_clips). It edits short vertical video clips of gaming moments, always credited to the original streamer and published with that streamer's permission, and publishes them to the accounts owned by the operator.

Clipclap is not a public service. Only the operator connects his own accounts to it. No other person is asked to sign in, and no data from other users is collected.

Streamer, rights holder, or viewer with a request (clip removal, question about your data)? Write to skybiness.clips@gmail.com.
