1. Who we are

Clipclap is a private tool operated by Skybiness Clips (the "Operator"). Contact: skybiness.clips@gmail.com.

2. Whose data is involved

Only the Operator's own accounts are connected to Clipclap. The tool does not ask any other person to sign in and does not collect data about viewers, followers or other users.

3. What is accessed

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (identifier, display name and avatar), through TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic), only to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to publish videos to that account (video.publish), and the equivalent permissions on Instagram and YouTube.
  • Videos produced by the Operator, with their title and description.

4. Storage

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored on the Operator's own computer, in a private configuration file that is not shared or published. Videos and publication logs stay on that computer.

5. Sharing and use

Data is used only to publish the Operator's videos to his accounts. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used for profiling.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the Operator revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time from the settings of each platform (for TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services), and tokens are then deleted. To ask about or delete data, write to skybiness.clips@gmail.com.

7. Children

Clipclap is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
